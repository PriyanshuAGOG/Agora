## Patterns worth copying (summary)

- **Scene length:** 2–4 s on average; one list or thought-cloud scene runs 6–11 s.
- **Hook → title:** the title card always starts between 3.8 s and 6.3 s.
- **Words on screen per scene:** 5–20; the frame is never a wall of text.
- **One "turn" word per episode** opens the second half: "But", "So naturally", "And just like that", "So I changed the plan".
- **Ending ladder:** CTA (keyword) or teaser → "See you tomorrow" → a Follow card styled differently from the previous days.
- **Continuity:** each hook references yesterday ("I listed ideas yesterday", "Okay enough research", "Apparently…"), so the series reads as one diary.
